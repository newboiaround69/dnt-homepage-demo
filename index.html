Questions we hear first

Anything else, ask us during the demo.

How much does a Dynamics 365 implementation cost?

Cost depends on the number of users, the Dynamics 365 modules you need, the DNT products you add, data migration and integrations. We give a fixed-scope estimate after a short discovery workshop, and you can get an early figure from our pricing calculator.

How long does an implementation take?

A single product added to an existing Dynamics 365 environment usually takes weeks. A full Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations rollout usually takes several months. We agree a phased plan with dates before work starts.

Which industries do you work with?

Our products and services focus on real estate developers and property managers, construction and contracting companies, and finance and investment teams in the UAE, KSA and the wider Middle East.
